# Aurora Textiles Pvt Ltd — Energy, Water & Waste Report 2025

## Energy Consumption

### Total Energy Use

| Year | Total Energy (MWh) | Renewable (MWh) | Renewable Share |
|------|-------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 2023 | 18,200            | 2,100           | 11.5%           |
| 2024 | 17,800            | 3,400           | 19.1%           |
| 2025 | 17,500            | 4,600           | 26.3%           |

Total energy consumption for FY 2025: 17,500 MWh, of which 4,600 MWh (26.3%) came from renewable sources.

### Energy Intensity

Energy intensity per tonne of fabric produced: 3.5 MWh/tonne (2025), down from 3.8 MWh/tonne in 2023.

### Renewable Energy Initiatives

- Rooftop solar: 2.4 MWp at Coimbatore (commissioned Q2 2024), 1.8 MWp at Surat (commissioned Q4 2024).
- Power purchase agreement for 5 MW wind energy signed in January 2025.
- Target: 50% renewable electricity by 2028.

## Water Management

### Water Withdrawal

| Year | Total Withdrawal (ML) | Recycled (ML) | Recycling Rate |
|------|-----------------------|---------------|----------------|
| 2023 | 145                   | 38            | 26.2%          |
| 2024 | 138                   | 48            | 34.8%          |
| 2025 | 132                   | 56            | 42.4%          |

Total water withdrawal for FY 2025: 132 megalitres (ML), with 56 ML recycled (42.4% recycling rate).

### Water Intensity

Water intensity per tonne of fabric: 26.4 kL/tonne (2025), down from 30.2 kL/tonne in 2023.

### Zero Liquid Discharge

Both the Surat and Coimbatore facilities operate zero liquid discharge (ZLD) systems. The Ahmedabad facility is scheduled for ZLD commissioning in Q3 2026.

## Waste Management

### Waste Generation

| Year | Total Waste (tonnes) | Hazardous (tonnes) | Diversion Rate |
|------|---------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| 2023 | 820                 | 45                 | 68.3%              |
| 2024 | 780                 | 38                 | 74.1%              |
| 2025 | 740                 | 32                 | 79.5%              |

Total waste generated in FY 2025: 740 tonnes, of which 32 tonnes hazardous. Waste diversion rate: 79.5%.

### Waste Reduction Initiatives

- Fabric scrap recycling program: 180 tonnes diverted to insulation manufacturing.
- Chemical waste reduction through closed-loop dyeing: 18% reduction in hazardous waste YoY.
- Target: 90% diversion rate by 2027, zero waste to landfill by 2030.
