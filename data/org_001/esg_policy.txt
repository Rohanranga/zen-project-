AURORA TEXTILES PVT LTD
ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE (ESG) POLICY
Effective Date: 1 April 2024
Version: 3.1

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes Aurora Textiles' commitment to sustainable business
practices across environmental stewardship, social responsibility, and
corporate governance. It applies to all operations, employees, contractors,
and supply chain partners.

2. ENVIRONMENTAL COMMITMENTS

2.1 Climate Action
- Reduce Scope 1+2 emissions by 30% by 2030 (base year 2023).
- Achieve 50% renewable energy share by 2028.
- Develop science-based targets aligned with 1.5°C pathway by 2026.

2.2 Water Stewardship
- Achieve zero liquid discharge at all facilities by 2027.
- Reduce water intensity by 25% by 2028 (base year 2023).
- Implement rainwater harvesting at all sites by 2026.

2.3 Circular Economy
- Achieve 90% waste diversion rate by 2027.
- Zero waste to landfill by 2030.
- Design 50% of product lines for recyclability by 2028.

3. SOCIAL COMMITMENTS

3.1 Workforce
- Living wage for all employees and contracted workers.
- 40% women in management by 2028.
- Zero tolerance for child labour and forced labour.
- Annual third-party audits of working conditions.

3.2 Community
- Allocate 2% of net profit to community development programs.
- Prioritise local hiring: 80% of workforce from within 50 km of facilities.
- Skill development programs for 500 community members annually.

3.3 Health & Safety
- Target: zero lost-time injuries.
- Mandatory safety training: 24 hours per employee per year.
- ISO 45001 certification at all facilities by 2026.

4. GOVERNANCE

4.1 Board Oversight
- ESG Committee reports directly to the Board of Directors.
- Quarterly ESG performance reviews.
- Independent ESG assurance by a third-party auditor annually.

4.2 Supply Chain
- All Tier 1 suppliers must meet Aurora Textiles' Supplier Code of Conduct.
- Annual supply chain ESG risk assessments.
- Target: 100% traceability of cotton sourcing by 2027.

4.3 Reporting & Transparency
- Annual sustainability report aligned with GRI Standards.
- CDP Climate Change and Water Security disclosures.
- Public disclosure of Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions.

5. REVIEW

This policy is reviewed annually by the ESG Committee and approved by the
Board of Directors. The next scheduled review is April 2025.
