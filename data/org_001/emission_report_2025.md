# Aurora Textiles Pvt Ltd — Greenhouse Gas Emission Report 2025

## Executive Summary

This report presents the greenhouse gas (GHG) emission inventory for Aurora Textiles Pvt Ltd for the fiscal year 2024–2025. Emissions are reported following the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard across all three scopes.

Aurora Textiles operates four manufacturing facilities in Gujarat and Tamil Nadu, producing cotton and blended fabrics for domestic and export markets.

## Scope 1 — Direct Emissions

Scope 1 covers direct GHG emissions from sources owned or controlled by Aurora Textiles, including on-site boilers, company vehicles, and fugitive emissions from refrigerants.

| Year | Scope 1 Emissions (tCO2e) | Change YoY |
|------|---------------------------|------------|
| 2023 | 1,420                     | —          |
| 2024 | 1,310                     | −7.7%      |
| 2025 | 1,250                     | −4.6%      |

Scope 1 (direct): 1,250 tCO2e for FY 2025.

The decline is attributable to the replacement of two coal-fired boilers at the Surat facility with natural gas units and the electrification of 40% of the vehicle fleet.

## Scope 2 — Indirect Emissions (Energy)

Scope 2 covers indirect emissions from purchased electricity, steam, and cooling consumed by Aurora Textiles.

| Year | Scope 2 Emissions (tCO2e) | Change YoY |
|------|---------------------------|------------|
| 2023 | 3,800                     | —          |
| 2024 | 3,600                     | −5.3%      |
| 2025 | 3,420                     | −5.0%      |

Scope 2 (indirect energy): 3,420 tCO2e for FY 2025.

The rooftop solar installation at the Coimbatore plant (2.4 MWp) offset approximately 1,200 MWh of grid electricity during the year.

## Scope 3 — Value Chain Emissions

Scope 3 includes all other indirect emissions across Aurora Textiles' value chain: raw material sourcing, logistics, employee commuting, and end-of-life treatment of products.

| Year | Scope 3 Emissions (tCO2e) | Change YoY |
|------|---------------------------|------------|
| 2023 | 9,500                     | —          |
| 2024 | 9,200                     | −3.2%      |
| 2025 | 8,900                     | −3.3%      |

Scope 3 (value chain): 8,900 tCO2e for FY 2025.

Key Scope 3 categories: purchased goods (cotton, dyes) account for 52%, upstream transportation 18%, and employee commuting 8%.

## Reduction Targets

Aurora Textiles has committed to a 30% reduction in Scope 1+2 emissions by 2030 (base year 2023) and a 15% reduction in Scope 3 by 2030.
