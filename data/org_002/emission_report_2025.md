# Meridian Logistics Ltd — Greenhouse Gas Emission Report 2025

## Executive Summary

This report presents the greenhouse gas (GHG) emission inventory for Meridian Logistics Ltd for the fiscal year 2024–2025. Emissions are reported following the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard across all three scopes.

Meridian Logistics operates a fleet of 320 trucks and 12 warehousing facilities across western and southern India, providing road freight and third-party logistics services.

## Scope 1 — Direct Emissions

Scope 1 covers direct GHG emissions from sources owned or controlled by Meridian Logistics, including the vehicle fleet, backup diesel generators, and refrigerant leaks from cold-chain units.

| Year | Scope 1 Emissions (tCO2e) | Change YoY |
|------|---------------------------|------------|
| 2023 | 3,150                     | —          |
| 2024 | 2,980                     | −5.4%      |
| 2025 | 2,810                     | −5.7%      |

Scope 1 (direct): 2,810 tCO2e for FY 2025.

The reduction is driven by the induction of 45 CNG-powered trucks and route optimisation software that reduced empty-run kilometres by 12%.

## Scope 2 — Indirect Emissions (Energy)

Scope 2 covers indirect emissions from purchased electricity consumed at Meridian Logistics' warehouses and offices.

| Year | Scope 2 Emissions (tCO2e) | Change YoY |
|------|---------------------------|------------|
| 2023 | 5,600                     | —          |
| 2024 | 5,350                     | −4.5%      |
| 2025 | 5,130                     | −4.1%      |

Scope 2 (indirect energy): 5,130 tCO2e for FY 2025.

LED lighting retrofits across 8 warehouses and a 3 MWp rooftop solar installation at the Pune distribution centre contributed to the reduction.

## Scope 3 — Value Chain Emissions

Scope 3 includes all other indirect emissions: subcontracted carriers, packaging materials, employee commuting, and capital goods.

| Year | Scope 3 Emissions (tCO2e) | Change YoY |
|------|---------------------------|------------|
| 2023 | 15,800                    | —          |
| 2024 | 15,100                    | −4.4%      |
| 2025 | 14,200                    | −6.0%      |

Scope 3 (value chain): 14,200 tCO2e for FY 2025.

Key Scope 3 categories: subcontracted road transport (48%), packaging (15%), employee commuting (10%), capital goods (9%).

## Reduction Targets

Meridian Logistics has committed to a 25% reduction in Scope 1+2 emissions by 2030 (base year 2023) and to transition 30% of the fleet to electric/CNG by 2028.
