MERIDIAN LOGISTICS LTD
ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE (ESG) POLICY
Effective Date: 1 January 2024
Version: 2.4

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes Meridian Logistics' commitment to sustainable logistics
operations, responsible labour practices, and transparent governance. It applies
to all business units, employees, owner-drivers, and supply chain partners.

2. ENVIRONMENTAL COMMITMENTS

2.1 Climate Action
- Reduce Scope 1+2 emissions by 25% by 2030 (base year 2023).
- Transition 30% of the fleet to electric or CNG by 2028.
- Achieve carbon-neutral last-mile delivery in metro cities by 2029.

2.2 Fleet Sustainability
- Route optimisation to reduce empty-run kilometres by 20% by 2027.
- Driver eco-training program: all drivers certified annually.
- Tyre pressure monitoring on 100% of fleet by 2026.

2.3 Warehousing
- 30% renewable electricity across all warehouses by 2028.
- LED lighting at 100% of facilities by 2026.
- Green building certification (IGBC) for all new warehouses.

3. SOCIAL COMMITMENTS

3.1 Workforce
- Fair wages aligned with regional living-wage benchmarks.
- 30% women in management by 2028.
- Zero tolerance for child labour, forced labour, and discrimination.
- Annual independent audits of working conditions.

3.2 Driver Welfare
- Health insurance for all owner-drivers and their families.
- Rest-stop network: 20 driver wellness centres by 2027.
- Maximum 10-hour driving shifts strictly enforced.

3.3 Community
- Road safety awareness programs in 50 communities annually.
- Skill development for 300 logistics professionals per year.
- Allocate 1.5% of net profit to CSR initiatives.

3.4 Health & Safety
- Target: zero fatalities, zero lost-time injuries.
- Mandatory safety training: 30 hours per employee per year.
- ISO 45001 certification at all warehouses by 2026.

4. GOVERNANCE

4.1 Board Oversight
- Sustainability Committee reports to the Board quarterly.
- ESG KPIs linked to executive compensation.
- Independent ESG assurance annually.

4.2 Supply Chain
- All subcontracted carriers must meet Meridian's Carrier Code of Conduct.
- GPS tracking and emission monitoring on 100% of subcontracted loads.
- Annual carrier sustainability scorecard assessments.

4.3 Reporting & Transparency
- Annual sustainability report aligned with GRI Standards.
- Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) reporting from 2025.
- Public disclosure of fleet emission intensity metrics.

5. REVIEW

This policy is reviewed annually by the Sustainability Committee and approved
by the Board of Directors. The next scheduled review is January 2025.
