# Meridian Logistics Ltd — Energy, Water & Waste Report 2025

## Energy Consumption

### Total Energy Use

| Year | Total Energy (MWh) | Renewable (MWh) | Renewable Share |
|------|-------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 2023 | 24,500            | 1,800           | 7.3%            |
| 2024 | 23,900            | 2,900           | 12.1%           |
| 2025 | 23,200            | 4,100           | 17.7%           |

Total energy consumption for FY 2025: 23,200 MWh, of which 4,100 MWh (17.7%) came from renewable sources.

### Energy Intensity

Energy intensity per million tonne-km of freight: 12.8 MWh/Mtkm (2025), down from 14.1 MWh/Mtkm in 2023.

### Renewable Energy Initiatives

- Rooftop solar: 3.0 MWp at Pune distribution centre (commissioned Q1 2024).
- Solar carports at Chennai and Bangalore hubs (1.2 MWp combined, Q3 2025).
- Target: 30% renewable electricity by 2028.

## Water Management

### Water Withdrawal

| Year | Total Withdrawal (ML) | Recycled (ML) | Recycling Rate |
|------|-----------------------|---------------|----------------|
| 2023 | 85                    | 12            | 14.1%          |
| 2024 | 80                    | 18            | 22.5%          |
| 2025 | 76                    | 24            | 31.6%          |

Total water withdrawal for FY 2025: 76 megalitres (ML), with 24 ML recycled (31.6% recycling rate).

### Water Intensity

Water intensity per warehouse: 6.3 ML/facility (2025), down from 7.1 ML/facility in 2023.

### Water Conservation

- Rainwater harvesting operational at 7 of 12 warehouse sites.
- Truck wash water recycling system at 4 depots.
- Target: 50% recycling rate by 2028.

## Waste Management

### Waste Generation

| Year | Total Waste (tonnes) | Hazardous (tonnes) | Diversion Rate |
|------|---------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| 2023 | 520                 | 68                 | 55.4%              |
| 2024 | 490                 | 58                 | 62.2%              |
| 2025 | 460                 | 50                 | 68.7%              |

Total waste generated in FY 2025: 460 tonnes, of which 50 tonnes hazardous. Waste diversion rate: 68.7%.

### Waste Reduction Initiatives

- Reusable pallet program: eliminated 120 tonnes of single-use packaging annually.
- Used oil recycling from fleet maintenance: 42,000 litres recovered.
- Tyre retreading program: 65% of fleet tyres retreaded at least once.
- Target: 80% diversion rate by 2027.
